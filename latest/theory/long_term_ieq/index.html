    Long-Term IEQ Evaluation (EN 16798-1, Annex B)¶

    Concept¶

    Instantaneous comfort scores answer "how is the room right now?" — long-term evaluation answers "for what fraction of the occupied time was the room acceptable?". EN 16798-1:2019 (which superseded EN 15251:2007) defines, in Annex B, a representation based on the percentage of occupied time spent in each of the four categories for every IEQ domain:

    Domain Cat. I Cat. II Cat. III Cat. IV
    Thermal Environment 20 % 68 % 7 % 5 %
    Indoor Air Quality 10 % 76 % 7 % 7 %

    Results are reported per category and cumulatively — Cat. I, Cat. I+II ("acceptable"), Cat. I+II+III, Cat. I+II+III+IV (= 100 %) — over the whole monitored period or per day/week/month.

    Category boundaries used in comfio¶

    Thermal — PMV (ISO 7730 / EN 16798-1)¶

    Category Criterion
    I |PMV| ≤ 0.2
    II |PMV| ≤ 0.5
    III |PMV| ≤ 0.7
    IV outside Cat. III

    The same bands are applied to sPMV/sPMVopt arrays as an approximation of the PMV-based classification (documented caveat, not a standard provision). They are also applied to TSV occupant votes by evaluate_temporal_tsv() — a reporting heuristic, since the bands are defined on PMV, a model prediction, not on subjective votes.

    Thermal — adaptive model (EN 16798-1)¶

    Classification on the absolute offset from the comfort temperature, \(|t_{op} - t_{comf}|\): Cat. I ≤ 2 K, Cat. II ≤ 3 K, Cat. III ≤ 4 K, Cat. IV outside.

    Indoor Air Quality — CO₂ above outdoor¶

    Categories are defined on the excess over outdoor CO₂, not on absolute ppm:

    Category CO₂ above outdoor
    I ≤ 400 ppm
    II ≤ 600 ppm
    III ≤ 1000 ppm
    IV > 1000 ppm

    This differs from evaluate_iaq(), whose thresholds are absolute-ppm practice indicators (ASHRAE Position Document on Indoor CO₂), not EN categories.

    Particulates & gases — WHO/EPA/WELL tiers¶

    EN 16798-1 does not define categories for PM2.5, PM10, TVOC, formaldehyde or CO. comfio maps the existing POLLUTANT_THRESHOLDS tiers (WHO Air Quality Guidelines, EPA NAAQS, WELL v2) onto the I–IV scale:

    Category Criterion
    I concentration ≤ excellent threshold
    II ≤ good threshold
    III ≤ moderate threshold
    IV above moderate (the poor tier is a scoring anchor, not a bound)

    These are health-based bands, not a standard provision — documented caveat. Custom bands can be passed via thresholds=.

    Visual and acoustic¶

    Default category limits follow typical office requirements in the EN 16798-1 annexes and are configurable per space/room type:

    • Visual (maintained illuminance): Cat. I ≥ 500 lux, II ≥ 300 lux, III ≥ 200 lux, IV below.
    • Acoustic (L_Aeq): Cat. I ≤ 35 dB, II ≤ 40 dB, III ≤ 45 dB, IV above.

    Aggregation rules in comfio¶

    • Occupancy: an optional occupancy mask drops unoccupied samples from every denominator.
    • Invalid samples: NaN labels (e.g. outside the PMV applicability domain) are excluded from the category percentages and reported separately as excluded_pct.
    • Time weighting: with timestamps, each sample is weighted by its actual duration (interval to the next sample), so irregular sampling does not distort the percentages; occupied hours are reported per category. Without timestamps, samples have equal weight.
    • Grouping: whole series by default, or any pandas offset alias (by="D", "W", "MS", …) for per-period tables.

    References¶

    • EN 16798-1:2019 — Energy performance of buildings — Ventilation for buildings — Part 1: Indoor environmental input parameters for design and assessment of energy performance of buildings addressing indoor air quality, thermal environment, lighting and acoustics, Annex B.
    • ISO 7730:2005 — PMV/PPD category limits (Table 2).
