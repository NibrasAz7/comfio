Limits of Applicability: This standard is only applicable to healthy individuals. This standard does not apply to occupants: a) whose clothing insulation exceed 1.5 clo; b) whose clothing is highly impermeable; or c) who are sleeping, reclining in contact with bedding, or able to adjust blankets or bedding.

The tool applies the ASHRAE 55-2023 relative-air-speed and dynamic-clothing corrections before evaluating PMV, and the Appendix H cooling effect (via SET) for elevated air speed.

Calculate comfort indexes in batch

Uploading datasets is handled by the Analyse my data workbench in this application: it reads CSV files and computes comfort indexes (PMV, PPD, SET, cooling effect) with the same pythermalcomfort model family used here.

Single-condition and two-condition evaluation are available on the ASHRAE-55, EN-16798 and Compare tabs of this tool; parameter sweeps are on the Ranges tab.
